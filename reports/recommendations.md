# Strategic Hospital Operations Recommendations
## Healthcare Operations & Patient Analytics Platform

> **Clinical & Data Disclaimer**: Recommendations herein reflect operational and capacity planning considerations derived from synthetic EHR modeling. They do not constitute clinical guidance or health policy directives.

---

## 1. Targeted Chronic Care Management for the Top 10% Spenders
- **Context**: The top 10% of patients account for 40.9% ($7.31M) of total network claim expenditures.
- **Action**: Establish dedicated nurse-led care management teams for patients with co-occurring hypertension, diabetes, and hyperlipidemia.
- **Expected Outcome**: A projected 15–20% reduction in avoidable emergency admissions within this cohort, saving an estimated $1.1M in uncompensated care costs.

---

## 2. Emergency Department De-escalation & Urgent Care Diversion
- **Context**: Over 18% of encounters enter through emergency departments, leading to protracted length of stay (median LOS 4.2 hours).
- **Action**: Implement triage fast-tracks diverting low-acuity cases (e.g. minor sprains, uncomplicated sinusitis) to co-located urgent care or same-day clinics.
- **Expected Outcome**: Alleviate ED physician burnout, reduce left-without-being-seen (LWBS) rates, and decrease median ED length of stay by 35 minutes.

---

## 3. Network Load Balancing Across Tertiary and Community Facilities
- **Context**: Central hospitals operate near peak utilization while community affiliate facilities operate at ~45% utilization.
- **Action**: Route ambulatory routine procedures (e.g. diagnostic imaging, routine lab draws) to community clinics via centralized scheduling.
- **Expected Outcome**: Improved overall resource utilization, reduced patient travel times, and optimized bed availability for acute inpatient admissions.

---

## 4. Upfront Patient Financial Navigation
- **Context**: Over $3.15M in cumulative patient out-of-pocket liability is distributed unevenly across uninsured and commercially insured patients.
- **Action**: Implement point-of-service eligibility checks and structured payment plan options at encounter registration.
- **Expected Outcome**: Accelerated collections and reduced bad-debt reserves.
